Stop and give me the big picture of this session. I may have lost track, so assume I remember nothing.

Base it on this conversation first. Check the repository (git status, branch, recent commits) only to confirm the current state, and don't start new work. If I gave a focus, stick to it: $ARGUMENTS

Use plain words and this shape, nothing more:

**TLDR:** one sentence on where we are.

**Goal:** what we're trying to do, in one or two lines.

**Done:** what's finished, a few bullets at most. Group small things together.

**In progress:** what's half done, and where it lives (files, branches, open pull requests). Say "nothing" if there's nothing.

**Not started:** what's left, in order.

**Needs you**
- Decisions waiting on me, approvals, failures, risks, or things I have to do by hand. Leave this section out if nothing needs me.

**Next:** the single next step, and whether it's mine or yours.

Keep the whole thing short enough to read in under a minute. Spell out acronyms and explain jargon the first time. If you're unsure about something, say so instead of guessing.
